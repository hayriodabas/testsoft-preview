Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026 · Effective Date: October 8, 2026
Official Guarantee: Data Not Collected All software published by TestSoft Lab (Hayri Odabaş, testsoftlab.com) operates strictly on-device. We run zero tracking infrastructure, integrate zero analytics SDKs, and never transmit your sensitive personal information to third-party endpoints.

1. The TestSoft Architecture Guarantee

TestSoft Lab builds dedicated native utilities for iOS, iPadOS, and macOS. Our engineering architecture operates on the principle of sovereign silicon: compute, analysis, and storage must terminate on your personal device.

2. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising

Across our entire application lineup:

  • Zero Analytics SDKs: No Firebase, Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, or similar behavioral telemetry software is compiled into our binary distributions.
  • Zero Ad Trackers: We do not integrate advertising SDKs, IDFA collection, or cross-app tracking networks.
  • No Account Gatekeeping: None of our applications require registration, logins, passwords, or cloud database profiling.

3. Specific Hardware Permissions & Zero Retention

Several instruments require system sensor access to perform calculations. In all cases, access is ephemeral and non-retained:

  • Camera Sensors (Plant Light Meter & Exposure): Used exclusively to sample instantaneous exposure values (aperture, ISO, exposure time) in volatile memory. No video or photographic frames are ever saved to local storage or transmitted across network interfaces.
  • Photo Library (Image Compressor & EXIF Remover): Accessed solely to read files explicitly selected by the user for on-device processing and to write resulting outputs back to Photos. Your imagery never leaves local device memory.
  • Audio & Speech Frameworks (Linecast & Voice to Text): Voice audio is processed entirely on the local Apple Neural Engine using Apple's on-device speech recognition models. Audio buffers are discarded immediately after transcript generation.
  • Microphone Input (Decibel Meter & Beatwise): Acoustic SPL and FFT calculations occur entirely in ephemeral memory buffers via Accelerate vDSP. No audio recordings are written or saved.

4. Financial Data & In-App Purchases

All transactions for optional lifetime pro upgrades are handled directly by Apple Inc. via the App Store (StoreKit 2). TestSoft has no visibility into or custody over your credit card details, billing address, or payment identities.

5. Apple App Store Privacy Nutrition Labels

In compliance with Apple App Store developer regulations, TestSoft's applications report: "Data Not Collected".
