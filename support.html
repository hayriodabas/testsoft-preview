License Restoration & StoreKit Purchases

How do I restore my lifetime purchase across new or multiple Apple devices?

All TestSoft pro unlocks are registered directly on Apple's StoreKit 2 infrastructure and bound to your Apple ID. We do not use proprietary accounts or licenses:

  • Launch the application on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.
  • Open Settings (or tap the padlock icon in the menu bar).
  • Tap Restore Purchases.
  • Apple's cryptographic receipt validator will verify your lifetime entitlement instantly.

Plant Light Meter: Lux & DLI Manual

Sensor Positioning & Calibration:

Lay your device flat where your plant's foliage is positioned, with the front or rear lens oriented toward the primary illumination vector (window or luminaire). Allow 1-2 seconds for automatic exposure convergence.

Understanding DLI (Daily Light Integral):

Lux measures instantaneous luminous flux perceptible to human vision. DLI (expressed in mol/m²/day) measures cumulative photosynthetically active radiation (PAR) absorbed over 24 hours. Input your observed photoperiod (hours of light) to compute DLI for daylight or specialized LED spectra.

Zero-Retention Camera Stream:

The app samples camera hardware metadata (ISO, exposure duration, aperture) in memory. No photographic frames are ever written to disk or transmitted over any network socket.

Linecast: Voice-Synced Teleprompter

Speech Pacing Mechanics:

Linecast integrates Apple's local on-device Speech framework. As you speak, word boundaries are matched in real time against your script, scrolling the canvas smoothly at your natural speaking cadence. If you pause, the prompter pauses.

Hardware Concurrency:

Video capture runs in a dedicated AVFoundation actor pipeline, ensuring 4K 60FPS video recording never stutters while speech recognition executes concurrently on the Neural Engine.

Beatwise: Sample-Accurate Timing Engine

Buffer-Level Clock Scheduling:

Most metronomes rely on software timers (like NSTimer or DispatchSource), which suffer from thread jitter and OS scheduling delays. Beatwise calculates click positions directly within the AVAudioEngine render graph, guaranteeing exact ±1 sample alignment (sub-millisecond precision).

Exposure: Film Photometry & Reciprocity Failure

Reciprocity Failure Calculations:

When shooting analog film at exposures longer than 1 second, silver halide emulsions experience reciprocity failure (the Schwarzschild effect). Exposure includes verified empirical compensation equations for Ilford (HP5+, FP4+, Delta) and Kodak (Tri-X, T-Max, Portra) stocks.

Decibel Meter: Acoustic Weighting & Calibration

dBA vs. dBC Weighting Curves:

dBA mimics the human ear's sensitivity to low-to-moderate sound levels by attenuating frequencies below 1 kHz. dBC provides a flatter response across the audible spectrum, ideal for measuring bass and industrial machinery.

Image Compressor Offline

Hardware Pipeline:

Utilizes Apple Silicon's hardware JPEG/HEIF image encoding blocks via CoreImage and ImageIO. All memory buffers are strictly bounded to prevent iOS memory warnings when processing multi-megabyte ProRAW photos.
